Day Board

Your whole school day, on one screen.

Day Board pulls your class schedule, Google Classroom, tests and calendar into one calm view, so you know what's next without opening five apps.

Your color code

Every class has its color, everywhere.

Pick a color for each subject once. The same bar follows the class through your schedule, its homework, its tests and the Next up card, so you can read the whole week at a glance.

Google Classroom

Homework sorted by when it's due.

Missing work sits in red at the top, then Due today, tomorrow, this week and later. Tests get their own countdown, pulled from Classroom and your calendar without duplicates.

What's new

New assignments show up on their own.

Every 30 minutes Day Board checks Classroom and your schedule, and tells you what was posted, which due dates moved, and which classes changed.

The F1 tab

Race weekends, in your time zone.

The next Grand Prix with a live countdown and every session time, the last podium, driver and team standings, and the full season. Tap a race for results, then jump into the F1 app.

Widgets and Mac

Glance without opening anything.

Home screen and lock screen widgets show your next class, the next test and what's missing, and they switch the moment each class starts. The same board runs as a Mac app, with two columns when the window is wide.

Make it yours

Your board, your order.

Drag tabs and sections into the order you think in, hide what you don't use, and add only what you want, like Due this week, Tests, Workouts or Reminders. Pick each class's color, and choose when your morning summary, class reminders and nightly due-tomorrow list arrive.

How it works

Built to read, never to change.

A small school script

It runs in your own school Google account and reads Classroom and your class schedule every 30 minutes from 8 AM to 9 PM. Only the app with your secret key can see the results.

Your calendar and reminders

Events you already see as classes or tests are hidden, so only the extras, like family plans, show up.

Read-only, on your devices

Day Board can't change, send or delete anything, and your sign-ins stay in your device's Keychain.
